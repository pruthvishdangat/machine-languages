Introduction

We work in the friction between industrial process and everyday ritual

industyr of the ordinary is a Chicago-based artist collective examining the unnoticed systems that structure our daily experience. Through installation, performance, and collaborative research, we reveal the hidden patterns embedded in ordinary objects, spaces, and gestures.

Our work exists in the gap—between the intentional and the accidental, the designed and the emergent, the industrial and the intimate. We don't seek to resolve these tensions, but to hold them, to make them visible, to let them speak.

Current Focus

Material Memory

Investigating how objects retain and transmit traces of their use—wear patterns, oxidation, thermal signatures, and the accumulated weight of handling.

Factory Rhythms

Translating industrial workflows into performance scores. What happens when the logic of assembly becomes choreography?

Infrastructural Intimacy

Finding the personal within systemic architecture—water meters, ventilation ducts, electrical panels as sites of unexpected tenderness.

Latest Work

Shift Patterns (2026)

A multi-channel sound installation documenting the acoustic signature of 24 hours inside a South Side warehouse. Eight speakers positioned throughout the gallery space emit overlapping recordings of machinery hum, forklift beeps, break room conversations, and the specific silence of 3am.

The piece reveals how industrial time differs from clock time—how the rhythm of labor creates its own temporal architecture, cyclical yet never quite repeating.
