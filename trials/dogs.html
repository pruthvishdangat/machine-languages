All About Dogs

Dogs are loyal, friendly, and playful animals that have been companions to humans for thousands of years. They come in many shapes, sizes, and breeds, each with their own unique personalities.

Why People Love Dogs

  • They are great companions and family members.
  • Dogs help keep us active and healthy.
  • They can be trained to help people with disabilities.
  • Dogs are known for their loyalty and affection.

Fun Facts

  • Dogs have an incredible sense of smell—up to 100,000 times better than humans!
  • The Basenji dog is known as the "barkless dog" because it doesn’t bark like other dogs.
  • There are over 340 different dog breeds worldwide.
